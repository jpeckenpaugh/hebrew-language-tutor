## Bugs handled

- **01-tts-issue** — TTS stops after first use; Hebrew reads "Exclamation Point".
  - Root cause: stray `!` in vocab id 1 data (`שלום!`), served/spoken verbatim;
    plus an unguarded `cancel()`-before-`speak()` making TTS unresilient to one
    bad utterance.
  - Resolution: data cleaned by re-seeding the DB; fail-safe hardening added to
    `speak()` (guarded `cancel()`, `onend`/`onerror`, punctuation strip).
  - Status: **Resolved**, archived to `bugs/resolved/01-tts-issue.md`.
