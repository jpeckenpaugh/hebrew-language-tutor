# Feature: Enlarged Centered Question

- **Sprint:** 03
- **Scope item:** f.)
- **Status:** capability definition

## Capability

In Quiz and Exam screens, the prompt "What is the Hebrew for: …?" is replaced by
the English word alone, centered on the page and enlarged to about 3× its
current size. The Hebrew answer options are enlarged to about 3× their current
size for readability.

## Scope Notes

- Covers both the enlarged centered question and the enlarged Hebrew answer
  options as a single capability.
- Applies to both Quiz and Exam modes.
