# Feature: Study Auto-Play

- **Sprint:** 03
- **Scope item:** d.)
- **Status:** capability definition

## Capability

The Study screen offers a green "play" control at the top right. Pressing it
speaks the current English term, pauses roughly two seconds, speaks the Hebrew
term, pauses roughly four seconds, then advances to the next vocabulary item and
repeats, until the end of the current lesson. Pressing the control again (now a
stop control) halts playback. In browsers without speech support, playback
advances on the same timing without audio.

## Scope Notes

- Operates within the current lesson only; it does not chain across lessons.
- A single control toggles between play and stop states.
